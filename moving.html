Important notice

Ujyalo is closing.
We've moved to Etayari.

Thank you for using Ujyalo. Over the past months it became clear the site needed to be rebuilt properly rather than patched — so that's what we did. Everything continues at etayari.app, and it's live now.

Go to Etayari → Free · takes about 30 seconds to sign up

Do I need to do anything?

Yes — one small thing. Etayari is a new site, so your Ujyalo login won't work there. Please create a free account on Etayari. It only takes a moment.

Key dates

  • NowEtayari is live. New sign-ups on Ujyalo are closed.
  • 15 Sept 2026Ujyalo goes offline. Accounts and saved practice history are deleted.
  • After thatujyalo.app automatically forwards to etayari.app.

What happens to my practice history?

It stays on Ujyalo until 15 September 2026, then it is permanently deleted along with your account. It does not carry across to Etayari.

If you'd like a copy of your history before then, email hello@ujyalo.app and we'll send it to you.

Is Etayari also free?

Yes. Past papers and practice stay free for students, exactly as they were on Ujyalo.

If anything here is unclear, just reply to the email we sent you or write to hello@ujyalo.app — a real person reads it.
